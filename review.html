How to read this dossier

Selection focuses on loss or corruption of work, access and consent boundaries, resource exhaustion, account and billing attribution, and failures that block essential workflows. Cosmetic-only complaints, identified duplicate labels, known fixed cases encountered in review, and several uncertain upstream-only cases were excluded. This is an editorial screen, not a formal severity assessment or an exhaustive audit.

All selected issue records were open when retrieved. A reproduction label, where present, is repository metadata, not proof that this review reproduced the behavior. Follow-up comments were fetched and screened for 217 selected reports; other entries explicitly show the remaining discussion coverage. Monetary losses, deleted-byte totals and prevalence claims are unverified unless separately established. This dossier does not establish organizational negligence or compare vendors.
